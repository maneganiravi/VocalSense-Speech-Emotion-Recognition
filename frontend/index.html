AUDIO PIPELINE
Feature information
One shared preprocessing pipeline keeps training and prediction consistent.

        

          
01
Audio input
Librosa loads uploaded audio as mono and resamples it to 22,050 Hz. Clips must be 0.5–30 seconds; this pipeline does not pad or crop them to a fixed duration.

          
02
CNN and CNN-BiLSTM features
Both models use 108 summary values: mean and standard deviation for 40 MFCCs, zero-crossing rate, RMS energy, and 12 chroma values.

          
03
Temporal experiments
Temporal and regularized models use a 64 × 54 feature sequence interpolated from MFCC, zero-crossing rate, RMS, and chroma frames.

          
04
Scaling and evaluation
Separate StandardScalers are fitted only on training actors. Evaluation uses fixed actor-independent train, validation, and test groups.
